### Dimitrios-Georgios Ntoulias

Mechanical and Aeronautical Engineering student at the University of Patras, working at the Laboratory for Manufacturing Systems and Automation (LMS).

My thesis work is on Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM / DED-Arc): thermal and optical monitoring of the process, defect detection with machine learning, and closing the loop so the process parameters adapt while the part is being built. The goal is a system that works on a real cell, not only in a paper.

Outside the lab I build software end to end: retrieval and LLM tools, real-time backends, and the mobile apps that sit on top of them. The repositories below are that work.

#### Research

- **Thermal and optical monitoring and control in WAAM** – melt pool and bead monitoring with thermal and optical cameras, ML-based defect detection, closed-loop control.
- **Multi-task deep learning perception for DED-Arc** – a multi-task U-Net (PyTorch Lightning, Hydra) whose outputs drive process parameter reconfiguration.

Thesis code is not public yet.
